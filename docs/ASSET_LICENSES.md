# Asset licences

Every file in `public/assets/` comes from a free pack and is **CC0 1.0** (public domain: free for any use,
commercial too, no credit required). We credit the authors anyway.

| Pack | Link | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit | CC0 1.0 (`models/kenney/License.txt`) |
| Poly Haven (textures, HDRI sky) | https://polyhaven.com | CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license) |
| ambientCG (textures) | https://ambientcg.com | CC0 1.0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) |

The files are made from the downloaded packs by `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs` (copies the models, resizes the
textures to 1024 px colour / normal and 512 px roughness, JPG quality 80) and `scripts/make-env.mjs` (the sky).
Poly Haven's asset pages name the photographer of each texture.

Not used (and not in the repo): the Quaternius Furniture Pack, the Industrial Pack (needs FBX conversion), and
the lift models (lifts come later).
